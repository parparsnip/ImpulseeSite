Delete your Impulsee account

Last updated 2 October 2026

Already deleted the app? Email us

Send an email to:

impulsee.app@gmail.com
  1. Send it from the email address you signed up with, with the subject Delete my Impulsee account. You don't need to give a reason.
  2. If you write from a different address, we'll ask you to confirm from your account's address first, so nobody can delete someone else's account.
  3. We'll delete the account within 30 days, usually much sooner, and reply to tell you it's done.

Still have the app? Do it there

  1. Open Impulsee and tap the settings cog.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account.
  3. Type DELETE and tap Delete for good.

It happens straight away, and you're signed out.

What gets deleted

Everything below is removed permanently, straight away, and can't be recovered:

  • Your login: your email address and password.
  • Your profile: your display name, your profile picture and your phone's notification token.
  • Every event you organised, open or past, along with everyone's "I'm in"s, updates and photos on it.
  • Your "I'm in"s and your updates on other people's events.
  • Every photo you uploaded: your profile picture, crew pictures and photos on updates.
  • Your place in every crew.
  • Any crew you're the last member of, with everything in it.

What isn't deleted

  • Crews other people are still in carry on without you, along with the events other people organised in them. If you uploaded a crew's picture, the crew goes back to a plain background.
  • Usage statistics and crash reports held by Google Firebase. These never include anything you typed, and are linked only to a random ID that no longer matches any account once yours is deleted. Firebase deletes them on its own schedule: up to 14 months for statistics and 90 days for crash reports.
  • Emails between you and us about the request, for as long as we need them to show it was handled.
  • Settings stored on your phone, if you still have the app installed. Uninstalling the app removes them.

For everything Impulsee collects and who handles it, see the privacy policy.
