What Impulsee is

Impulsee lets you create or join crews (groups of friends), post events ("shouts") to let your crew know you're heading out, and see who's coming. That's it — it's a coordination tool, not a social network.

Your account

  • You need an email address and a password to sign up. You're responsible for keeping your password safe.
  • One account per person. Don't share your login or let someone else use your account.
  • The display name your crews see starts as the part of your email before the @. You can change it in Settings.
  • You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete account, or by emailing us. See the account deletion page for exactly what gets removed.

Age requirement

Impulsee is for people aged 18 and over. By signing up you confirm you are at least 18. If we learn someone under 18 is using the app, we'll delete their account.

What you can do

  • Create and join crews with an invite code.
  • Post shouts with a venue, a time, an energy level and an optional note.
  • Post updates (text and photos) on open events.
  • Tap "I'm in" to say you're coming, or back out while the event is still open.
  • Use venue search (optional) to find nearby pubs and bars when posting a shout.
